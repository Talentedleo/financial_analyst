## 1. NYT — Interesting Times with Ross Douthat (Jun 2025)

**Format:** Podcast, 62 minutes
**Link:** [NYT](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/07/11/podcasts/interesting-times-a-mind-bending-conversation-with-peter-thiel.html)

**Key Topics:**

### The Stagnation Thesis (2025 Update)
- "Yes, I still broadly believe in the stagnation thesis" — Thiel confirms his long-standing view
- Technology progress has been slower than expected since the 1970s
- The exception: information technology (computers, internet) — but this is one sector
- Biotechnology, energy, transportation, materials — all underdelivered
- The question: are we dreaming big enough?

### AI Ambition Critique
- "Is AI too modest an ambition?"
- Thiel argues AI development focuses too narrowly on mimicking human cognition
- The deeper question: what should we be building?
- Contrasts AI with more ambitious goals: space colonization, life extension, radical energy

### On Elon Musk and Mars
- Discusses Musk's Mars ambitions critically — "Does Elon even want to go to Mars?"
- Musk's importance: he forces people to think about big futures
- Even if Mars colonization doesn't happen, the attempt produces valuable technological spillovers

### Political Evolution
- Reflects on Trump (2016) and whether the bet paid off
- "Did Trump live up to Thiel's expectations?" — nuanced critique
- Broader critique of Silicon Valley's political culture
- Discussion of "regime question" — what kind of political system enables innovation?

### Life Extension and Science
- Thiel has long been interested in life extension research
- "Does taking more scientific risk include life extension?"
- Views aging as a solvable engineering problem, not an inevitability

## 8. Key Conversation Themes Across All Interviews

1. **Stagnation Thesis:** Progress has slowed dramatically since the 1970s — with the exception of IT
2. **Monopoly Good, Competition Bad:** The purpose of business is to escape competition
3. **Definite Optimism:** The future requires specific beliefs about what will happen
4. **Secrets Exist:** Important truths that few people believe are waiting to be discovered
5. **Founder Paradox:** Great founders are strange, and their strangeness is their superpower
6. **Technology as Escape:** Only technology can solve the stagnation problem
7. **Question Authority:** "The most contrarian thing is not to oppose the crowd but to think for yourself"
8. **Bigger Ambition Needed:** AI is good but not ambitious enough — we need Mars, life extension, radical energy
