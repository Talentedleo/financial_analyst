# 06-timeline.md — Peter Thiel's Life and Career Timeline

---

## Early Life

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1967 | Born October 11 in Frankfurt, West Germany |
| 1968 | Family emigrates to US; lives in Cleveland, Ohio |
| 1971-1977 | Family moves to South Africa, then South West Africa (Namibia) |
| 1977 | Family settles in Foster City, California |
| 1985 | Valedictorian of San Mateo High School |
| 1985-1989 | Attends Stanford University; BA in Philosophy |
| 1987 | Co-founds The Stanford Review (conservative/libertarian newspaper) |
| 1989 | Graduates from Stanford |
| 1989-1992 | Stanford Law School; JD degree |

---

## Early Career

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1992 | Law clerk for Judge James L. Edmondson (11th Circuit) |
| 1993 | Law clerk for Justice Anthony Kennedy (Supreme Court) |
| 1993-1994 | Securities lawyer at Sullivan & Cromwell (7 months — quits) |
| 1994 | Speechwriter for Education Secretary; derivatives trader at Credit Suisse |
| 1995 | Co-authors The Diversity Myth with David Sacks |
| 1996 | Founds Thiel Capital Management |

---

## PayPal Era

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1998 | Co-founds Confinity with Max Levchin and Luke Nosek |
| 2000 | Merges with Elon Musk's X.com; becomes PayPal |
| 2001 | Appointed CEO of PayPal |
| 2002 | PayPal IPO (February); sold to eBay (October) for $1.5B |
| 2002 | Personal profit from sale: ~$55 million |

---

## Investing and Empire Building

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2003 | Founds Palantir Technologies with Alex Karp, Stephen Cohen |
| 2004 | $500,000 investment for 10.2% of Facebook (first outside investor) |
| 2005 | Launches Founders Fund with Ken Howery, Luke Nosek |
| 2006 | Palantir shifts focus to government contracts |
| 2007 | Gawker outs Thiel as gay |

---

## Growth and Expansion

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2010 | Co-founds Valar Ventures |
| 2011 | Launches Thiel Fellowship (pay students to drop out) |
| 2011 | Founds Thiel Capital |
| 2011 | Granted New Zealand citizenship |
| 2012 | Co-founds Mithril Capital |
| 2012 | Teaches CS183 at Stanford (lecture notes become Zero to One) |
| 2014 | Publishes Zero to One (September 16) |
| 2015 | Part-time partner at Y Combinator (until 2017) |
| 2016 | Secretly funds Hulk Hogan's Gawker lawsuit; Gawker bankrupt |
| 2016 | Speaks at RNC endorsing Donald Trump |
