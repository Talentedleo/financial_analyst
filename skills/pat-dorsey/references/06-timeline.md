# Pat Dorsey — Timeline

A chronological overview of key events in Pat Dorsey's life and career.

---

## Pre-Morningstar (Before 1999)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **~1970** | Born (exact date uncertain — Pat Dorsey is private about personal details) |
| **~1992** | Graduates from university; begins career in finance |
| **~1995** | Earns CFA charterholder designation |
| **1997–1999** | Early career roles in equity research before joining Morningstar |

---

## Morningstar Era (1999–2008)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **~1999** | Joins Morningstar, Inc. as an equity analyst |
| **2000s** | Rises to become Director of Stock Analysis / Director of Equity Research; manages a team of 90+ equity analysts |
| **2003–2004** | Plays integral role in developing the **Morningstar Rating for Stocks** (the star rating system for equities) |
| **2004** | Publishes *The Five Rules for Successful Stock Investing* (Wiley) — Morningstar's comprehensive guide to stock analysis covering moats, valuation, and portfolio management |
| **2004–2006** | Leads development of **Morningstar Economic Moat Rating** system — the institutional framework that categorizes companies as Wide Moat, Narrow Moat, or None |
| **2008** | Publishes *The Little Book That Builds Wealth: The Knockout Formula for Finding Great Investments* (Wiley) — a focused, accessible distillation of the moat framework |
| **2008** | Speaks at Google (Talks at Google series) about moat investing |

---

## Sanibel Captiva Trust Era (2008–2013)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **~2008** | Leaves Morningstar to become Director of Research at **Sanibel Captiva Trust Company** (Florida-based wealth management firm) |
| **2008–2013** | Applies moat analysis framework to portfolio management in a wealth management context |
| **2010** | Appearances at industry conferences (Morningstar Investment Conference, CFA Society events) |

---

## Dorsey Asset Management Era (2013–Present)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **2013** | Founds **Dorsey Asset Management LLC** — his own investment firm focused on long-term, moat-driven investing |
| **2013–2020** | Builds concentrated portfolio of wide-moat companies; maintains very low public profile |
| **2020–2023** | AUM grows to approximately **$750 million** (as of June 2023) |
| **2023** | Quartr publishes detailed profile: "Pat Dorsey: Economic Moats and Beyond" — summarizing his career and investment philosophy |
| **2024** | Portfolio continues to be tracked by StockCircle and other 13F tracking services |
| **2024–2025** | Holdings: Meta, Alphabet, Disney — consistent with moat-focused, concentrated strategy |
| **2026** | Continues managing Dorsey Asset Management with no public interviews or media appearances |
