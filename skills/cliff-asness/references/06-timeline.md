# 06-timeline.md — Cliff Asness's Life and Career Timeline

---

## Early Life and Education

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1966 | Born October 17 in Queens, New York City |
| 1970 | Family moves to Roslyn Heights, Long Island |
| 1984 | Graduates from Herricks High School, New Hyde Park, NY |
| 1988 | Graduates summa cum laude from University of Pennsylvania (M&T Program: BS in Computer Science + BS in Finance from Wharton) |
| 1989 | Enrolls at University of Chicago Booth School of Business |
| 1991 | Receives MBA with high honors from UChicago; starts summer job at Goldman Sachs Asset Management |
| 1994 | Receives PhD in Finance from University of Chicago; dissertation on momentum and value strategies under Eugene Fama and Kenneth French |

---

## Career at Goldman Sachs

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1991 | Summer internship at Goldman Sachs Asset Management |
| 1992-1994 | Works part-time at Goldman while completing PhD |
| 1994 | Fama unhappy with Asness's decision to stay at Goldman; Asness starts building Goldman's quant research desk |
| 1995 | Persuades Goldman partners to provide $10M initial investment for computer-driven models |
| 1996 | Initial $10M fund grows to $100M; Goldman opens Global Alpha fund to the public |
| 1997 | Global Alpha becomes one of the largest quant hedge funds; Asness and team develop multi-factor models for currencies, bonds, and equities |
| 1997 | Publishes "The Interaction of Value and Momentum Strategies" in JPM — seminal paper on combining factors |

---

## AQR Capital Management

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1998 | Co-founds AQR Capital Management with David Kabiller, John Liew, and Robert Krail |
| 1998 | AQR launches with ~$1B AUM — one of the largest hedge fund launches at the time |
| 1999 | Marries Laurel Elizabeth Fraser |
| 2000 | AQR enters traditional portfolio management (long-only mandates) |
| 2001 | AQR reaches ~$750M AUM |
| 2003 | Publishes "Fight the Fed Model" — debunks the popular Fed Model |
| 2004 | AQR moves headquarters from New York to Greenwich, Connecticut; reaches $12B AUM |
| 2005 | Opens first international office in Australia |
| 2005 | NYT publishes detailed profile of Asness |
| 2007 | Global Alpha (Goldman) at peak: $12B AUM; AQR grows significantly |

---

## The 2008 Crisis and Recovery

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2007 | AQR AUM peaks at ~$39B |
| 2008 | AQR AUM declines to ~$17B during financial crisis; massive factor drawdowns |
| 2009 | AQR launches first family of alternative mutual funds (liquid alts) |
| 2009 | Publishes several papers defending factor investing post-crisis |
| 2010 | AQR AUM recovers to ~$33B |
| 2010 | Featured in Scott Patterson's *The Quants* |

---

## Growth and Expansion

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2011 | Opens UK office in London |
| 2012 | Publishes "Leverage Aversion and Risk Parity" in FAJ |
| 2013 | Publishes "Value and Momentum Everywhere" in Journal of Finance — landmark paper on cross-asset factor consistency |
| 2013 | Publishes "The Devil in HML's Details" in JPM |
| 2014 | AQR partners with London Business School to establish The AQR Asset Management Institute ($15M donation over 10 years) |
| 2014 | Publishes "Quality Minus Junk" — defines the quality factor systematically |
| 2015 | AQR establishes QUANTA Academy learning and development program |
| 2015 | Wins Bernstein Fabozzi/Jacobs Levy Award |
| 2016 | Connecticut extends $35M in financial aid to keep AQR in state (540 jobs retained, 600 new jobs created) |
| 2016 | Bloomberg reports AQR's alternative funds top rivals with 17% returns |
| 2017 | AQR AUM peaks at ~$224B; Forbes notes Asness moving toward lower-fee mutual funds |
| 2018 | AQR annual profit plunges 34%; announces job cuts |
| 2019 | AQR AUM declines to ~$193B; market headwinds for factor strategies |
