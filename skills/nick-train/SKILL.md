# Nick Train

Co-founder of Lindsell Train Ltd. Manager of Finsbury Growth & Income Trust. "The Franchise Fan." One of the UK's most renowned investors with a 40+ year track record.

This skill captures Nick Train's complete investment framework — his focus on brand equity, "eternal franchises," concentrated portfolios, permanent equity philosophy, and valuation evolution.

---

## Introduction

Nick Train graduated in History from Oxford University and began his career at GT Management, where he was influenced by Alex Dundas's legendary 12-stock Japanese fund with zero turnover — a powerful real-world validation that concentrated, patient investing works. He also studied Peter Lynch and naturally gravitated toward the Buffett/Munger approach. — a powerful real-world validation of the Buffett approach.

After GT was acquired and a brief stint at M&G (just 18 months before Prudential took over), Train co-founded Lindsell Train Ltd with Michael Lindsell in 2000. He has managed the Finsbury Growth & Income Trust since 2000.

His approach is characterized by: concentrated portfolios of 25–30 stocks, "forever" holding periods, a focus on businesses with powerful brand equity, and an evolving valuation framework that now embraces capital-light digital franchises alongside traditional consumer brands.

---

## Core Philosophy

### Brand Equity — "Eternal Franchises"
Train seeks **eternal franchises** — businesses with brand power that consumers love and trust. Key characteristics:
- Strong brand equity creating pricing power
- Global distribution and recognition
- "No across the street" — no adequate substitute
- Long history of high returns on capital

### Permanent Equity — "Ideal Holding Period is Forever"
> "My ideal holding period is forever."

Influenced by Buffett and Alex Dundas, Train believes the best businesses should never be sold. Holding "forever" allows compounding to work its magic.

### Concentrated Portfolio
- 25–30 stocks maximum
- "Wandering through a desert for years before gloriously happening on a monolith"
- Contrast with Anthony Bolton's "turning over pebbles on a beach" approach
- High conviction in each position

### Valuation Evolution
Train's framework has evolved significantly:
- **Traditional:** Max 30x P/E (3% earnings yield) for 20th century businesses
- **Current:** Willing to pay up to 50x P/E for capital-light digital franchises
- "21st century business models can and ought to be valued differently"
- "Exceptional companies deserve exceptional multiples"
