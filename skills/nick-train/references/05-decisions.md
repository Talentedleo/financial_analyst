# Nick Train — Key Investment Decisions

---

## 1. Co-founding Lindsell Train (2000)
After GT was acquired and M&G was acquired by Prudential, Train (age 40) decided to found his own firm with Michael Lindsell. Motivated by: "I wanted to create an environment where whatever happened to the business, it was going to be my decision."

## 2. Diageo — Forever Holding
One of Train's longest-held positions. Premium spirits with global brand power, pricing power, and "no across the street." He describes it as a "forever holding."

## 3. Rightmove & Auto Trader — Digital Winners (2023–2024)
Recent additions reflecting Train's evolved valuation framework. Capital-light digital platforms with extraordinary economics (75%+ operating margins at Rightmove). Willing to pay 50x P/E for these — a departure from his traditional 30x max.

## 4. Exiting Non-UK Positions (2024–2025)
Following UK market underperformance, Train exited all non-UK holdings to focus entirely on UK-listed equities. "The ridiculous underperformance of the UK stock market" prompted strategic reorientation.

## 5. Raising Personal Stake (2025)
Train increased personal FGT holding to 4.04% (£52M). Demonstrates conviction during challenging period. Second top-up in a year.

## 6. Continuation Vote (2026)
Facing a shareholder continuation vote after 5 years of underperformance. A test of his philosophy and patience.
