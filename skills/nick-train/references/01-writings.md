## 2. Core Investment Philosophy

### Brand Equity — "Eternal Franchises"
Train seeks businesses with powerful, enduring brands that consumers love and trust. His ideal businesses have:
- Strong brand equity that creates pricing power
- Global distribution and consumer recognition
- "No across the street" — no adequate substitute
- Long history of returns on capital

### Permanent Equity — "Ideal Holding Period is Forever"
> "My ideal holding period is forever." — Nick Train

Influenced by Buffett and GT colleague Alex Dundas (who ran a 12-stock Japanese fund with zero turnover), Train believes that the best businesses should never be sold.

### Concentrated Portfolio
- 25–30 stocks only
- High conviction in each position
- "Wandering through a desert for years before gloriously happening on a monolith"

### Valuation Evolution
Train's thinking has evolved:
- **Traditional:** Max 30x P/E (3% earnings yield)
- **Current (2025):** Willing to pay 50x P/E for capital-light digital franchises like Rightmove
- Frameworks evolve: "21st century business models can and ought to be valued differently"

## 4. Recent Performance & Challenges (2025–2026)

Finsbury Growth & Income Trust has experienced a challenging period:
- **10-year NAV total return:** +107.7% vs FTSE All-Share +90.4% (still outperforming over long term)
- **2025 YTD:** Share price +4.3%, NAV +6% vs benchmark +11.5%
- **Discount to NAV:** ~8.4%
- **Continuation vote scheduled** — test of shareholder patience
- Train raised personal stake to 4.04% (£52M) — "skin in the game"

### Strategic Shift (2024–2025)
- Exited all non-UK positions
- Reorienting toward "digital winners" — data-driven growth companies
- **New positions:** Rightmove (+16% return), Auto Trader
