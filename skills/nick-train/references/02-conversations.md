# Nick Train — Conversations & Interviews

---

## 1. Behind the Balance Sheet — "The Franchise Fan" (Sep 2025) ⭐

**Format:** 1hr+ podcast interview
**Host:** Stephen Clapham
**Source:** [Behind the Balance Sheet #51](https://behindthebalancesheet.com/podcasts-singles/51-the-franchise-fan/)

**Key Content:**
- Detailed discussion of investment philosophy
- "Ideal holding period is forever"
- Evolution of valuation framework: from 30x max to 50x for digital franchises
- Why Rightmove and Auto Trader represent new thinking
- The "desert vs pebbles" analogy (contrasting his approach with Anthony Bolton)
- Diageo as a "forever holding"
- History degree as preparation for investing
- Experience at GT Management (Alex Dundas's Japanese fund influence)
- Why he left GT and M&G to set up Lindsell Train

**Memorable Quotes:**
> "I've always been motivated to build really long-term track records, which involves staying at one place for really long periods of time."

> "There is a cohort of capital-light digital franchises where maybe I would feel that a warranted valuation should be an earnings yield of two — which is 50 times earnings."

> "Buying an engineering company in 1985 at below book value — Rightmove at low 20 times earnings was as significant a value access point."

## Summary of Conversational Themes

1. **Eternal franchises** — Brands and businesses with enduring competitive advantages
2. **Forever holding period** — The ideal: never sell the best businesses
3. **Concentration** — 25–30 stocks; high conviction
4. **Valuation evolution** — Willing to pay more for digital/asset-light franchises
5. **UK focus** — Concentrated on UK-listed equities
6. **Humility** — Acknowledges recent underperformance
7. **Skin in the game** — Personal stake of £52M in own fund
