# 06-timeline.md — Nick Sleep's Life & Career Timeline

> Note: Sleep is intensely private. Pre-Nomad biographical details are limited. The timeline is primarily focused on the Nomad years (2001–2014) and post-Nomad activities.

---

## Early Career (Pre-2001)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **c. 1964** | Born in England. Exact date and location not publicly known. |
| **Late 1980s** | Graduated from university (subject and institution undisclosed). |
| **c. 1990** | Moved into investment management. Worked at several firms including Baron's Asset Management. |
| **1990s** | Built a strong reputation as a concentrated, long-term value investor. Developed early versions of what would become the SES framework. |
| **c. 1999** | Met Qais Zakaria. The two began discussing the idea of starting their own investment partnership. |

---

## Nomad Investment Partnership (2001–2014)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **2001** | **Launch:** Nomad Investment Partnership founded by Nick Sleep and Qais Zakaria. Zero-fee, zero-lockup partnership structure. Sleep invested his entire net worth. |
| **2001** | **First letter:** Sets out partnership philosophy — concentrated, long-term, contrarian. |
| **2002** | Returned +48.2% in a down market. |
| **2002** | Warned partners not to extrapolate one-year returns. |
| **2003** | Articulated the concentration thesis: "Diversification is a protection against ignorance." |
| **2004** | **The SES Letter:** Introduced Scale Economies Shared. Costco identified as the archetypal SES business. |
| **2004** | Began building the Costco position (would become Nomad's largest holding). |
| **2005** | Introduced "Gazelles and Elephants" framework for position sizing. |
| **2006** | Introduced "portfolio curvature" thinking. |
| **2007** | Average holding period exceeded five years. |
| **2008** | **Financial Crisis:** Held all positions. Added to some. Wrote candidly about mistakes. |
| **2008** | Began investing in Amazon, recognizing it as the ultimate SES company. |
| **2009** | Portfolio recovered strongly. Bought more during the panic. |
| **2010** | Began investing in CarMax based on SES thesis. |
| **2010** | Joined "Q&A with Zak" on the Corner of Berkshire and Fairfax forum — a rare public engagement. |
| **2011** | Articulated the Amazon SES flywheel in detail. |
| **2012** | Introduced "geographic franchise" framework. |
| **2013** | Cumulative return exceeded 800%. Final letter foreshadowed the close. |
| **2014** | **Close:** Nomad returned all capital to partners. Cumulative return: 921% vs. 118% for MSCI World. Sleep effectively retired from professional investing at the peak of his career. |

---

## Post-Nomad (2014–Present)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **2014** | Disappeared from public life. Gave no interviews. Attended no conferences. |
| **c. 2015** | Established the IGY Foundation (named after Donald Fagen's song about the International Geophysical Year). Focus on charitable giving. |
| **2015** | Sleep's only known statement after closing: "I had enough. More than enough." |
| **2021** | William Green's *Richer, Wiser, Happier* published — the first and only long-form profile of Sleep. |
| **2024** | Founders Podcast #365 devoted an entire episode to reading Sleep's letters. |
| **2026** | IGY Foundation website active. nomadletters.com redirects to igyfoundation.org.uk. Sleep has not spoken publicly in over a decade. |
