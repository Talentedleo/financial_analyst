# 06 — Timeline

A complete timeline of Joel Greenblatt's life and career.

---

## Early Life

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1957 | Born December 13 in Great Neck, New York |
| 1979 | B.S. summa cum laude from Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania |
| 1980 | M.B.A. from Wharton School. Published paper "How the small investor can beat the market" with Pzena and Newberg in Journal of Portfolio Management |
| 1980-1981 | Briefly attended Stanford Law School, then dropped out |
| 1981 | Joined Halcyon Investment as analyst (risk arbitrage / special situations) — took job at half the average MBA salary to learn from experienced partners |

---

## Gotham Capital (1985-1995)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1985 | Founded Gotham Capital with $7 million (mostly from Michael Milken). Focus: special situations investing. |
| 1985-1994 | Gotham Capital produced 50% annualized returns (before GP fees), ~30% net. Grew from $7M to ~$500M. |
| 1989 | Robert Goldstein joined Gotham Capital as partner |
| 1995 | Returned all outside capital (~$500M) — strategy capacity reached. Operated as family office. |

---

## Private Capital & System Development (1995-2008)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1997 | Published *You Can Be a Stock Market Genius* — Simon & Schuster |
| 2000 | Invested $1M for 25% of Michael Burry's Scion Capital |
| 2002 | Donated $2.5M to P.S. 65Q in Queens, NY |
| 2002-2006 | Taught value investing as adjunct professor at Columbia Business School (continuing for 20+ years overall) |
| 2005 | Published *The Little Book That Beats the Market* — New York Times bestseller, 300,000+ copies sold |
| 2006 | Co-founded Success Academy Charter Schools (then Harlem Success Academy) |
| 2006 | Gotham's investment in Scion Capital had grown to $100M; Gotham exited |

---

## Gotham Asset Management (2008-Present)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2008 | Gotham Asset Management, LLC created as successor to Gotham Capital's advisory business |
| 2010 | Published *The Little Book That Still Beats the Market* (updated edition) |
| 2010 | Launched four conventional mutual funds raising $360 million |
| 2011 | Published *The Big Secret for the Small Investor* |
| 2011 | Gotham Assets Under Management: ~$5.73B (peak) |
| 2018 | AUM declined to ~$3.74B |
| 2020 | Published *Common Sense: The Investor's Guide to Equality, Opportunity, and Growth* |
| 2021 | AUM: $3.74B (latest confirmed figure) |
| 2021 | Interview with Rich Pzena — discussed value opportunity set, market froth |
| 2024 | Money Maze Podcast interview — discussed Apple case study, margin of safety, evolution of approach |
| 2026 | Gotham Asset Management 13F filings continue to show active portfolio management |

## Key Milestones

- **1985-1994:** 9-year track record of 50% annualized returns at Gotham Capital
- **2005-Present:** Magic Formula invested by thousands of individual investors worldwide
- **2000:** Early backer of Michael Burry (The Big Short)
- **2006:** Founded what became NYC's largest charter school network
- **20+ years:** Teaching value investing at Columbia Business School
- **Current role:** Managing Principal and Co-CIO, Gotham Asset Management

---

## Personal Life

- Born and raised in Great Neck, New York (Long Island)
- Three children
- Co-founded Value Investors Club with John Petry (prestigious 250-member community)
- Founded the $1M Gotham Prize for Cancer Research (2007-2008)
- Board service: Institute for Student Achievement, Davidson School of Jewish Theological Seminary
- Current age: 68 (born December 13, 1957)
