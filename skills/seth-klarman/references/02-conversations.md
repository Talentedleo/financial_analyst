# 02 — Conversations

Seth Klarman is intensely private. He rarely gives interviews — perhaps 3-5 substantive ones in his entire career. Below are the most significant known conversations.

---

## Major Interviews

### 1. Goldman Sachs Interview with John Waldron (July 24, 2025)
**Host:** John E. Waldron, Goldman Sachs President & COO  
**Source:** Chinese financial media (full translated transcript available)

**Conversation Overview:**
One of Klarman's most revealing public conversations. Over 30 minutes, he systematically laid out his investment philosophy, current portfolio positioning, and views on risk.

**Key Excerpts:**

*On Margin of Safety:*
> "The core idea of margin of safety is 'reserving space for mistakes you might make.' The world is full of uncertainty. Buying assets at a sufficient discount creates a buffer against the unexpected."

*On the Value of a Good Business:*
> "First you must accurately determine value. Second, you need to consider the path to value realization — is there a catalyst? How will value be unlocked? My understanding has evolved over time, but I have never deviated from the margin of safety principle."

*On Risk Assessment:*
> "When the team submits an investment proposal, I often ask: if we look back in two years and this investment failed, what will we be discussing as the cause? This forces everyone to identify major risks in advance and ensure they are already reflected in the current valuation."

*On Portfolio Construction:*
> "We build protection through multiple dimensions: hedging positions, holding cash to preserve purchasing power in extreme markets, senior debt instruments that have structural priority, put options in private investments. When the storm comes, these protections often let us stand out."

*On Baupost's Current Positioning (2025):*
- Private investments: 20-24%
- Credit investments: ~20%
- Equities: ~20%
- Real estate: ~15%
- Hedging tools: few percentage points
- Cash: ~10%

*On Commercial Real Estate:*
> "Commercial real estate's deadlock is beginning to crack. There is a large wall of debt maturing, which will trigger a wave of selling. This is exactly where we are focusing our deployment."

*On Private Credit:*
> "Private credit seems like a fragile area. It hasn't really been tested yet — not by rising interest rates, not by the bond market's unusual behavior on down days."

*On Buffett:*
> "The value investing discipline is the perfect anti-blowup discipline. Buffett's legend isn't just about returns — it's about the miracle of never blowing up."

*On Patience:*
> "I'd rather lose half my clients than lose half my clients' money."

*On Analyst Attachment:*
> "Analysts date the idea, portfolio managers marry the philosophy." (His famous quote on the difference between analysts who fall in love with positions and portfolio managers who maintain objectivity)

*On Market Timing:*
> "We know the market will turn. We don't know when or how severely. So we build an all-weather portfolio — acceptable at any point, never trapped."

*On Cognitive Biases:*
> "All humans share cognitive biases — loss aversion, anchoring, etc. Great investors can restrain these instincts."

*On the 2008 Crisis:*
> "When the smartest competitors exit, that is our buying opportunity. Panic days are also golden communication days — people who are shaken are more willing to share their views."

**Portfolio Breakdown (July 2025):**

| Asset Class | % of Portfolio | Notes |
|-------------|---------------|-------|
| Private Investments | 20-24% | Private equity and debt |
| Credit / Distressed | ~20% | Baupost's historically best-performing area |
| Public Equities | ~20% | Reduced exposure; had been unprofitable |
| Real Estate | ~15% | Increasing — sees most opportunity here |
| Hedging | Few % | Protecting against downside |
| Cash | ~10% | Down from 30%+ in earlier years; strategic reserves |

**Distressed Exposure Evolution:**
- 2018-2019: ~5% of AUM
- 2023: ~24%
- 2025: ~20% — maintained elevated level

**Additional 2025 Insights (from Jan 2025 media coverage):**

*On Baupost's Decade of Underperformance (2014-2024):*
- Annualized return: ~4% — far below historical 20%
- Three years of losses (none exceeding -5%)
- Caused by: zero interest rate environment, sustained bull market that disadvantaged value strategies
- Clients redeemed ~$7 billion over three years

*On the 2024 Restructuring:*
- Largest in Baupost's 42-year history
- Cut ~20% of investment team
- Refocused on core strengths: distressed debt, event-driven equities, private investments
- "Through streamlining the investment team, we have increased energy levels, focus, accountability, and collaboration efficiency." (year-end letter)

*On Performance Recovery:*
- 2025 return: ~10% (first double-digit since 2021)

*On Coinbase Investment:*
- Baupost bought convertible bonds from Coinbase
- "We bought Coinbase securities based on their financial health — $5B cash, debt far below that. This is a bet on the company's financial strategy, not a bullish bet on Coinbase stock."

*On Cryptocurrency:*
- "Crypto is just a tempting idea with almost nothing substantial behind it. It only enjoyed speculative enthusiasm driven by low interest rates."

*On Market Rating:*
- "On a scale of 1 to 10, the current US financial market scores about a 4."

*On Liquidation Value Investing (from earlier Klarman writings):*
Klarman is famous for seeking liquidation value situations — stocks trading below net asset value with tangible assets worth more than the market price. His framework for assessing this:
- Orderly liquidation > fire sale (gives better value)
- Cash = $1 on the dollar
- Accounts receivable ≈ face value
- Inventory depends on nature (supermarket ≠ warehouse of computers)
- Intangibles = 0 (zero value in liquidation)

## Key Themes Across All Known Conversations

### 1. Margin of Safety Above All
Klarman's single most consistent theme. Every conversation returns to it.

### 2. "I'd Rather Lose Half My Clients..."
His most famous quote, repeated in multiple contexts. The principle: prioritize capital preservation over retention or growth.

### 3. No Leverage
Consistently emphasized as the single most important risk control measure.

### 4. Patience and Cash
- Willing to hold large cash positions for years
- Returned $4B to investors in 2015 rather than invest at subpar returns
- "The greatest anxiety is finding no good ideas"

### 5. Catalysts Matter
Unique focus among value investors — doesn't just buy cheap, buys cheap WITH a path to value realization.

### 6. Market Structure Creates Opportunity
Institutional constraints (benchmarks, redemption terms, liquidity requirements) create forced selling or forced buying that patient capital can exploit.
