# 06 — Timeline

A complete timeline of Seth Klarman's life and career.

---

## Early Life

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1957 | Born May 21 in New York City. Jewish family. |
| 1963 | Family moved to Baltimore, MD near Pimlico Race Course — sparked lifelong love of horse racing |
| ~1967 | At age 10, purchased first stock: one share of Johnson & Johnson (reasoning: "I used a lot of Band-Aids"). Stock split 3-for-1, tripled his investment. |
| 1979 | Graduated Cornell University, magna cum laude, economics major (math minor planned, switched) |
| 1979 | Summer internship at Mutual Shares fund — introduced to Max Heine and Michael Price |
| 1979-1981 | Worked at Mutual Shares for 18 months before business school |
| 1982 | MBA from Harvard Business School, Baker Scholar. Classmates included Jeffrey Immelt, Jamie Dimon, Steve Burke, Stephen Mandel. |

---

## Baupost Group Era

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1982 | Founded Baupost Group with Harvard professor William Poorvu and partners. Starting capital: $27M. Starting salary: $35,000/year. Name is acronym of founders' last names. |
| 1982-2008 | Baupost builds legendary track record: ~20% annualized return over 26 years |
| 1991 | Published *Margin of Safety: Risk-Averse Value Investing Strategies for the Thoughtful Investor* |
| 2007 | Warned about housing bubble, held large cash position, avoided subprime |
| 2008 | Opened Baupost to new investors, raised $4B during crisis. Bought up to $100M/day in distressed assets. Portfolio returned -7% to -13% for the year. |
| 2009 | CIT Group bonds at 65 cents / Sallie Mae bonds double-digit returns / FacetBiotech at $9/share |
| 2012 | Purchased $456M in Lehman Brothers bonds (4 years after collapse) |

---

## Later Years

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2013 | Held 40% cash; appeared on 60 Minutes (rare TV interview); contributed footnotes to Howard Marks's *The Most Important Thing Illuminated* |
| 2015 | Returned $4 billion to investors — "few opportunities worth pursuing" |
| 2017 | Warned about cryptocurrency speculation |
| 2019-2020 | COVID crisis — deployed capital into distressed credit |
| 2023 | Commercial real estate commentary — "The deadlock is beginning to crack"; distressed exposure reached ~24% |
| 2023 | Sohu/Financial media coverage: "Rating current US markets: 4/10" |
| 2024 | Largest restructuring in Baupost history: cut ~20% of investment team, refocused on core strategies |
| 2025 (Jan) | Media reports: 2014-2024 returns only ~4%; clients redeemed ~$7B over 3 years; AUM declined to ~$22B |
| 2025 (Jul) | Goldman Sachs interview with John Waldron — rare 30-minute public conversation, detailed portfolio breakdown |
| 2025 | ~10% return for the year — first double-digit since 2021 |

## Key Milestones

- **1982-Present:** 44+ years at Baupost Group — one of the longest-tenured hedge fund CEOs
- **Only 5 losing years in 42 years** — worst loss -12%
- **2008:** Inducted into Institutional Investor Alpha's Hedge Fund Manager Hall of Fame
- **2017:** Ranked #15 highest-earning hedge fund manager by Forbes. Net worth: $1.3B.
- **The $1,000+ Book:** *Margin of Safety* out of print since 1991, sells for $1,000-$2,500+ on secondary market
