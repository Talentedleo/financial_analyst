# Li Lu — Timeline

A chronological overview of key events in Li Lu's life and career.

---

## 1966–1989: Early Life in China

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1966** | Born April 6 in Tangshan, Hebei Province, China |
| **1966–1976** | Cultural Revolution; parents sent to labor camps by the government; transitions through multiple orphanages and caretakers |
| **1976** | Survives the Tangshan earthquake (magnitude 7.8, ~250,000 deaths) — one of the deadliest earthquakes in recorded history |
| **1985** | Enrolls at Nanjing University, initially studying physics; later transfers to economics |
| **Spring 1989** | Becomes a student leader in the Tiananmen Square protests; serves as deputy commander of the Tiananmen Square Command Post and press spokesman for the Democracy Movement; participates in hunger strike |
| **June 1989** | Flees China after the crackdown; goes to France, then to New York City at age 23 |

---

## 1990–1996: Columbia University & Early Career

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1990** | Publishes *Moving the Mountain: My Life in China* |
| **1990–1996** | Studies at Columbia University: American Language Program → School of General Studies → Columbia College → Columbia Law School → Columbia Business School |
| **1993** | Attends Warren Buffett's lecture at Columbia University; decides to pursue a career in value investing; begins investing in stocks while still a student |
| **1996** | Graduates as one of the first in Columbia's history to receive three degrees simultaneously: B.A. in Economics (Columbia College), J.D. (Columbia Law School), MBA (Columbia Business School) |
| **1996** | Begins career as corporate finance associate at Donaldson, Lufkin & Jenrette (investment bank) |

---

## 1997–2003: Founding Himalaya Capital & Early Years

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1997** | Founds Himalaya Partners (early version of Himalaya Capital); initially operates as a hedge fund with long-short strategies |
| **Late 1990s** | Successfully navigates the dot-com bubble by adhering to value investing discipline |
| **2003** | Meets Charlie Munger on Thanksgiving Day; they talk for 4-5 hours, walking through every investment Li Lu had researched |
| **2003** | Munger decides to invest $88 million of personal family wealth with Li Lu — the only outside manager Munger has ever invested with |

---

## 2004–2010: Transformation & Breakthrough

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **2004** | Munger helps Li Lu restructure Himalaya Capital into a partnership model (no management fees, long-term committed capital) |
| **2004** | New fund launched with Munger's $50 million + Li Lu's own capital; achieves ~36% CAGR over 5 years |
| **2002–2004** | First invests in BYD as a cornerstone investor |
| **2008** | Recommends BYD to Munger and Buffett; Berkshire Hathaway invests $230 million for 10% stake |
| **2010** | Accompanies Warren Buffett on China visit; evaluates investment opportunities for Berkshire |
| **2010** | Publishes foreword for Chinese edition of *Poor Charlie's Almanack*; helps translate and publish in China |
| **2010** | Wall Street Journal publishes "From Tiananmen Square to Possible Buffett Successor" — worldwide attention |
| **2010** | Withdraws himself from consideration for Berkshire Hathaway investment management role |

---

## 2011–2020: Growth & Philanthropy

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **2011** | Named World Economic Forum Global Leader for Tomorrow |
| **2012** | Receives Columbia College John Jay Award |
| **2012** | Receives Raoul Wallenberg Human Rights Award |
| **2016** | Featured in Smithsonian National Museum of American History's "Family of Voices" exhibition |
| **2017** | Appointed as Trustee of Columbia University |
| **2020** | Publishes *Civilization, Modernization, Value Investing and China*; Charlie Munger writes the foreword |
| **2020** | Book sells 279,000+ copies in China; rights sold to Traditional Chinese, Korean, and Thai |
| **2020** | Co-founds Guardians of the Angeles Charitable Foundation for COVID-19 relief |
| **2020** | Elected to American Academy of Arts and Sciences |

---

## 2021–Present: Asian American Advocacy & Recent Developments

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **2021** | Co-founds The Asian American Foundation (TAAF) with Jerry Yang, Joseph Tsai, and others; $125M initial commitment; serves as Board Chair |
| **2023** | Himalaya Capital AUM estimated at $14 billion |
| **2023** | FT publishes profile: "No other investor has a life story quite as unbelievable as Li Lu" |
| **2023–2024** | Berkshire Hathaway gradually reduces BYD stake after 15+ years |
| **2024** | Steps down as Columbia University trustee (served 2017–2024) |
| **2024** | TAAF has raised over $1 billion for AAPI communities |
| **November 2024** | Gives rare interview on first anniversary of Munger's death — first interview with Chinese media in 4 years |
| **2025** | Donates $15 million to Columbia Law School; library renamed "The Li Lu Law Library" |
| **2025** | Appointed as Trustee of California Institute of Technology (Caltech) |
| **2025–2026** | Multiple investment analyses published about his track record (Shipra, Washington Morning, Maalzone) |
