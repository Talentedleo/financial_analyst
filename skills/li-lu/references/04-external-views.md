# Li Lu — External Views

A collection of how Li Lu is perceived and described by others — media, peers, mentors, and industry observers.

---

## 1. Charlie Munger's View

Munger's public statements about Li Lu carry exceptional weight given Munger's legendary skepticism and reluctance to delegate capital.

**Key Quotes:**
- "Li Lu is the only outside manager I have ever invested with." — Reuters (2019)
- "Li Lu is a genius, and we've made a lot of money together." — Interview (2010)
- Described Li Lu as "China's Warren Buffett" / "Chinese Warren Buffett" — Multiple occasions
- Called him a "foregone conclusion" to manage Berkshire's investment portfolio post-Buffett — Wall Street Journal (2010)

**The $88 Million Bet:** Munger invested $88 million of his personal family wealth with Li Lu after their first conversation in 2003. This grew to approximately $400-500 million, and Himalaya Capital now manages $14 billion in assets.

**The BYD Connection:** Munger credited Li Lu for discovering BYD. At the 2024 Berkshire shareholder meeting, Buffett recalled that Munger only "slammed the table" twice to insist on investments — one of which was BYD, brought to him by Li Lu.

## 2. Warren Buffett's View

- Buffett has publicly acknowledged Li Lu's role in the BYD investment
- Li Lu was rumored to be a potential successor for Berkshire's investment portfolio
- Buffett invited Li Lu to accompany him on his 2010 China visit to evaluate investment opportunities

## 3. Columbia Business School — Bruce Greenwald

Professor Bruce Greenwald, a leading value investing authority and Ben Graham scholar, has hosted Li Lu at Columbia for interviews and discussions. Greenwald's platform has provided Li Lu with a venue to articulate his investment philosophy to the academic community.

## 4. Media Profiles

### Financial Times — Eleanor Olcott (2023)
> "No other investor has a life story quite as unbelievable as Li Lu"

The FT profile explores his journey from Tangshan earthquake survivor and Tiananmen student leader to Charlie Munger's trusted money manager. It highlights his unique perspective spanning both Chinese and Western markets.

**Source:** [FT Profile](https://www.ft.com/content/5308cd9f-037e-4524-a6d8-7388b3514199)

### Wall Street Journal — Susan Pulliam (2010)
> "From Tiananmen Square to Possible Buffett Successor"

This influential profile broke the story that Li Lu was being considered for a role managing Berkshire Hathaway's portfolio. It detailed his improbable journey and the depth of Munger's trust in him.

**Source:** [WSJ Article](https://www.wsj.com/articles/SB10001424052748703977004575393180048272028)

### Smithsonian National Museum of American History
Li Lu is featured in the "Family of Voices" exhibition — an honor that places his personal story within the broader narrative of American immigration and achievement.

**Source:** [Smithsonian](https://americanhistory.si.edu/family-voices/individuals/li-lu)
