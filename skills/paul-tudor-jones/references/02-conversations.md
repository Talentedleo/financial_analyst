## 1. CNBC Squawk Box Interview (May 7, 2026) — 1 Day Old!

**Format:** Live TV interview, ~21 minutes
**Link:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/video/2026/05/07/watch-cnbcs-full-interview-with-tudor-investment-corporation-founder-and-cio-paul-tudor-jones.html)

**Key Topics:**

### AI Bull Market Has "Another Year or Two to Run"
- PTJ compares Claude (Jan 2026) to Microsoft in 1981 — a transformative technology at the start of its cycle
- Compares current AI phase to 1995 when internet commercialization accelerated with Windows 95
- "Those were both the beginning of productivity miracles that lasted four to five and a half years. We're kind of, I'd say, 50 or 60%. If I had to pick a period, we've got another year or two to run."

### Parallels to 1999
- In terms of bull market sentiment, this "continues to feel like the 1999 period"
- When it ends, the drawdown "could be significant"
- "Just imagine the stock market went up another 40%. The stock market GDP is going to probably be good lord 300%, 350%. You just know that there'll be some breathtaking kind of corrections."

### He's Still Buying AI
- "I'm a macro trader, so I just buy baskets"
- Has recently added to AI investments
- "I love always to find historical precedents"

### AI Risk Warning
- Governments will "ultimately need to step in with regulation"
- Worried about AI becoming "dangerous to humanity if left unchecked"

### Robin Hood Foundation Update
- 2026 focus is on food insecurity

**Link:** [CNBC Article](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/07/paul-tudor-jones-says-ai-bull-market-has-another-year-or-two-to-run.html)

## 2. Invest Like The Best — Patrick O'Shaughnessy (April 2026)

**Format:** Long-form podcast, ~71 minutes
**Link:** [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/gr/podcast/paul-tudor-jones-lessons-from-50-years-in-markets/id1154105909?i=1000763993727)

**Key Topic: "Lessons From 50 Years in Markets"**

### Trading vs. Investing: The Fundamental Distinction
- PTJ explicitly contrasts himself with Warren Buffett
- "Our fund has a minus 0.12 correlation with the S&P 500 over 40 years. So 100% of our returns are alpha."
- "I feel like I've been a right guard in the NFL for 50 years, fighting in the trenches every day"

### On Market Valuations
- "We're over-equitized as a country. We have the highest individual equity weightings in the history of the country."
- "We're 252% of stock market cap to GDP. In 1929 we were 65%. In 1987 we got to ~85-90%. In 2000, 170%."
- "If you buy the S&P at this current valuation, the 10-year forward return is negative when you buy the S&P with a PE of 22. That's what history shows."

### On AI Risk, Bubbles and Buffett
- Legendary trader discusses his 50-year career in markets and his philosophy on life
- Contrasts the current AI-driven market with past technology booms
- Compares his trading approach to Buffett's long-term investing approach

### On His Constant Bearishness
- A Wealth of Common Sense analysis notes: "Paul Tudor Jones is a trader, not a long-term investor"
- His natural disposition is bearish — it's a personality trait that makes him good at his job
- "The perfect investment strategy is the one you stick with come hell or high water"

**Link:** [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/gr/podcast/paul-tudor-jones-lessons-from-50-years-in-markets/id1154105909?i=1000763993727)

## 3. Market Wizards — Jack Schwager (1989) — The Definitive Interview

**Format:** Book interview
**Link:** *Market Wizards* by Jack D. Schwager

**Key Topics:**

### On Risk Control
- "Ninety percent of any great trader is going to be the risk control"
- Risk management is the defining characteristic of great traders
- Learned through painful early losses in his 20s

### On the 5:1 Rule
- Targets 5:1 reward-to-risk ratio
- This allows 80% loss rate while being profitable
- Finding asymmetric opportunities is the central skill

### On Being a "Slave to the Tape"
- Price action > fundamentals
- Technical analysis is primary
- The younger generation is "hampered by the need to understand and rationalize"

### On Emotional Detachment
- No loyalty to positions
- View past trades as history
- "If you have a losing position making you uncomfortable, get out"

### On the Evolution of a Trader
- Simplicity → Complexity → Informed Simplicity
- "Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication"

## 8. Key Conversation Themes Across All Interviews

1. **Risk Management is Everything:** "Ninety percent of any great trader is risk control" — repeated in every interview
2. **Technical Analysis Primary:** Price action matters more than fundamentals; "slave to the tape"
3. **Macro Framework:** Top-down approach starting with global economic picture
4. **5:1 Asymmetry:** The most important quantitative rule — 5x reward for 1x risk
5. **No Ego:** "The second you think you're good, you're dead"
6. **Historical Analogies:** Every market situation has a historical parallel
7. **Trader vs. Investor:** PTJ explicitly distinguishes his trading approach from long-term investing
8. **Constructive Paranoia:** Fear is an asset — "I have to be frightened to be successful"
