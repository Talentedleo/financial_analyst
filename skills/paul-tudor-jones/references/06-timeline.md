# 06-timeline.md — Paul Tudor Jones's Life and Career Timeline

---

## Early Life

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1954 | Born September 28 in Memphis, Tennessee |
| 1972 | Graduates from Memphis University School |
| 1972-1976 | Attends University of Virginia; economics degree; welterweight boxing champion; Sigma Alpha Epsilon president |
| 1976 | Graduates with BA in Economics from UVA |

---

## Early Career

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1976 | Starts at New York Cotton Exchange; mentored by Eli Tullis; fired for sleeping at desk |
| 1976-1980 | Commodities broker at E.F. Hutton & Co.; makes $1M+ in commissions in second year |
| 1980 | Founds Tudor Investment Corporation at age 26 |
| 1980s | Floor trader at NY Cotton Exchange — only one losing month in 3.5 years |
| 1986 | Serves as treasurer of New York Cotton Exchange |
| 1986 | Adopts a sixth-grade class in Bedford-Stuyvesant after watching 60 Minutes |

---

## The 1987 Crash and Legend Status

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1987 | Predicts Black Monday; short market heavily; Tudor Futures Fund returns 62% in October |
| 1987 | Full-year return: 125.9% after fees; earns estimated $100 million |
| 1987 | Becomes a legend at age 33 |
| 1988 | Co-founds Robin Hood Foundation |

---

## 1990s: Expansion

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1990 | Shorts Japanese equities as bubble bursts; returns 87.4% |
| 1990 | Pleads guilty to wetlands violation; $1M fine + $1M restitution |
| 1991 | Closes Tudor Select Fund, returns investor capital |
| 1992-1995 | Chairman of New York Cotton Exchange |
| 1993 | Co-founds Everglades Foundation |
| 1994 | SEC settlement ($800,000 fine) for uptick rule violation |

---

## 2000s: Conservative Shift

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2000s | Tudor returns "dim" according to NYT — deliberately trading more conservatively |
| 2002 | Leases Grumeti reserve in Tanzania's Serengeti |
| 2006 | NYT profiles PTJ as "American conservationist" for Serengeti work |
| 2008 | Tudor survives financial crisis; lower volatility era |

---

## 2010s: Philanthropy + Evolution

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2012 | Co-invests in Castleton Commodities International (LDH Energy acquisition) |
| 2013 | Co-founds JUST Capital |
| 2016 | Awarded Audubon Medal for conservation work |
| 2019 | Joins Giving Pledge with wife; Forbes estimates net worth at $5.3B |

---

## 2020s: Bitcoin, AI, and Latest Chapter

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2020 | Buys bitcoin as inflation hedge — "own the fastest horse" |
| 2022 | Tudor manages ~$12B AUM |
| 2024 | Forbes net worth estimate: $8.1 billion |
| 2026 | April: Invest Like The Best podcast — "Lessons From 50 Years in Markets" |
| 2026 | May 7: CNBC Squawk Box — "AI bull market has another year or two to run" |
| 2026 | May 7: Robin Hood Gala — focus on food insecurity |
