# Chuck Akre — External Views

A collection of how Chuck Akre is perceived and described by others.

---

## 1. Investment Masters Class

> "Charles Akre, or 'Chuck' for short, runs his namesake fund, the $6B Akre Capital, out of a 'one-traffic light' town in Virginia. Away from the hullaballoo of Wall Street, Chuck and his team spend their days searching for attractively priced 'compounding machines'; rare businesses that have contributed to Akre's long history of market beating returns."

**Source:** [Investment Masters Class](http://mastersinvest.com/newblog/2018/8/3/chucks-3-legged-stool)

---

## 2. StableBread Analysis

> "Charles 'Chuck' Akre manages ~$11.8B from a small town in rural Virginia, far removed from the frenzy of Wall Street. His team dedicates their time to hunting for what they call 'compounding machines,' and their track record speaks for itself (1.2% outperformance over 16 years)."

**Source:** [StableBread](https://stablebread.com/three-legged-stool/)

---

## 3. The Acquirer's Multiple (Jan 2026)

> "Chuck Akre's latest 13F reveals a portfolio that continues to embody his long-standing investment philosophy: own a concentrated basket of durable compounders, let management teams create value over time, and intervene only at the margins."

> "Akre isn't trying to predict the macro — he's letting quality do the work."

**Source:** [The Acquirer's Multiple](https://acquirersmultiple.com/2026/01/chuck-akre-quality-compounding-through-trim-discipline/)

---

## 4. Mohnish Pabrai on Akre's Letters

Mohnish Pabrai, another famed value investor, has publicly praised Chuck Akre's annual letters as among the best in the industry, placing them alongside letters by Buffett, Munger, and Markel's Tom Gayner.
