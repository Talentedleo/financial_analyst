# Chuck Akre — Conversations & Interviews

A curated collection of Chuck Akre's interviews, speaking engagements, and key conversations about his "Three-Legged Stool" framework and compounding philosophy.

---

## 1. MOI Global / Latticework — Intelligent Investing 100 (2021/2025)

**Format:** In-depth interview conducted by Alex Gilchrist of MOI Global
**Date:** Originally 2021; republished on Latticework in January 2025
**Source:** [Latticework by MOI Global](https://www.latticework.com/p/chuck-akre-on-winning-big-by-investing)

**Context:** Part of the "Intelligent Investing 100" series — a classic interview with superinvestor Charles T. "Chuck" Akre, Jr. One of the most detailed conversations available about his investment framework.

**Key Themes:**

### The Three-Legged Stool Explained
> "In my office I had an old-fashioned three-legged milking stool, and it just hit upon me one day as an idea for making it easier for people to follow how we thought about making investments."

- **Leg 1 — Business Model:** Is it a high-return business? What causes the high return? Is it getting better or worse?
- **Leg 2 — Management:** Not only have they done a terrific job, but they've acted in ways that treat investors as partners.
- **Leg 3 — Reinvestment:** History of reinvestment of free cash flow, quality of opportunities, length and breadth of the runway.

> "When we have those things — we'd call it investment nirvana if we had five stars in each one — then the question concerns what you pay for it."

### On Learning and Experience
> "Good judgments come from experience, and experience comes from bad judgments. This whole business, this odyssey is a process. It's about trying to discover pieces of information or factors that could have some better-than-even odds of predictability about how an investment return will work for you."

### On Stock Options as Hidden Dilution
Akre highlighted stock options as a significant but often overlooked cost:
> "You get these big, rapidly growing companies that are darlings of the market. It just seems like it's nothing that they give away 2% or 3% per year in options. But the fact is they're giving away 2% or 3% of your capital per year."

### On Simplicity
> "Everything should be made as simple as possible, but not simpler." (Einstein quote, written on the crown molding in Akre's boardroom)

### On Earnings Momentum vs. Business Quality
> "Earnings momentum drives the investment world. That's evidenced when you hear everybody speak about whether they missed by a penny or beat by a penny. We don't think that's a particularly good way to go about this. We're interested in trying to identify businesses with high returns on the owner's capital."

## 3. Akre Capital Quarterly Letters / Fund Commentary

While not interviews in the traditional sense, Akre's quarterly commentaries to the Akre Focus Fund (AKRIX) shareholders serve as his most consistent form of communication.

**Notable excerpts (from recent letters):**

**On AI Hype (2024 Q2 letter):**
> "We're on the same page and don't take recent returns as a proxy for true shareholder value creation. Distinct market sentiment and relative under- or outperformance from your companies' true financial strength and compounding."

**On Selling:**
> "Number one is, when we find really good ones our experience has been it's been a mistake to sell them even at very high valuations, you just live through it."

**On His Personal Holdings (up 200,000%):**

## Summary of Conversational Themes

Across all conversations, these themes consistently emerge:

1. **Compound returns are everything** — "All investing is ultimately about rate of return"
2. **Three-Legged Stool is non-negotiable** — All three legs must be present
3. **Reinvestment is the most critical leg** — "Over a period of years, our thinking has focused more and more on the issue of reinvestment as the single most critical ingredient"
4. **Ignore short-term noise** — Don't trade on earnings "beats" or "misses"
5. **Concentration is a feature, not a bug** — "Diversification is protection against ignorance"
6. **Patience is a competitive advantage** — "If you have an investment that has gone up 50 times, the next time it doubles it's now up 100 times"
7. **Quality protects on the downside** — "The practice of not losing money is significantly advanced by the selection of superior businesses"
8. **Management integrity matters enormously** — "Once a guy's put his hand in your pocket, he'll do it again"
