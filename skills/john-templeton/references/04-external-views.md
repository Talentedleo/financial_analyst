# John Templeton — External Views

---

## 1. Money Magazine (1999)
> "Arguably the greatest global stock picker of the century"

## 2. Time Magazine (2007)
Named to Time 100 list under "Power Givers" — cited his "pursuit of spiritual understanding, often through scientific research"

## 3. Charlie Rose
"I intended to live out my life in the Bahamas and felt obligated" — on citizenship

## 4. Investopedia
Describes Templeton as a pioneer of global value investing who "refused to limit his search for bargains to US borders"

## 5. Franklin Templeton
The firm continues to use Templeton's name and philosophy as a cornerstone of its value-oriented fund family

## 6. Common Descriptors
- "The greatest global stock picker of the century"
- "Pioneer of emerging market investing"
- "Contrarian value investor"
- "Bargain hunter extraordinaire"
- "Philanthropist and spiritual explorer"
- "Sir John" (knighted 1987)

## 7. Legacy
- Templeton Growth Fund outperformed global index by 3% annually for his entire career
- Outperformed by 6% annually after moving to the Bahamas (distanced from Wall Street noise)
- The Templeton Prize: £1M+ annual award (deliberately set above Nobel Prize value)
- John Templeton Foundation: $1B+ endowment funding science-religion research
- Templeton College, Oxford (now Green Templeton College)
