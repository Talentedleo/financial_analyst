# John Templeton — Conversations & Interviews

A curated collection of Sir John Templeton's interviews, speeches, and recorded conversations about his investment philosophy and philanthropic vision.

---

> **Note:** Templeton (1912–2008) was a pre-internet era investor. Most recorded conversations are from his later years (1980s–2000s). Modern sources include Franklin Templeton materials and biographies.

---

## 1. Charlie Rose Interview (1997)

**Format:** One-on-one television interview
**Source:** PBS Charlie Rose Show

**Key Themes:**
- Discussed his investment philosophy and the "points of maximum pessimism" concept
- Explained why he moved to the Bahamas (not for tax reasons — he claimed Bahamas had higher taxes)
- Discussed renouncing US citizenship: "I intended to live out my life in the Bahamas and felt obligated"
- Emphasized that discipline and patience are more important than intelligence in investing

---

## 2. Templeton's 16 Rules for Investment Success

**Format:** Published guide by Franklin Templeton
**Source:** [Franklin Templeton PDF](https://www.franklintempleton.com/retail/pdf/home/splash_PUB/TL_R16_1207.pdf)

Templeton's most widely distributed "conversation" with investors — a set of distilled rules covering investing philosophy, behavioral discipline, and life wisdom.

**Notable quotes from the rules:**
- "The four most dangerous words in investing: 'This time it's different.'"
- "Invest — don't trade or speculate"
- "No one knows everything — humility is essential"

---

## 3. Interviews in "Global Investing: The Templeton Way" (1990s)

**Format:** Book-length series of interviews
**Author:** Norman Berryessa

A collection of interviews with Sir John Templeton covering his global investment philosophy in depth. Topics include:
- Why he invested in Japan in the 1960s
- How he identified European bargains after WWII
- His views on emerging markets
- The discipline of global bargain hunting

---

## 4. Money Magazine Profile (1999)

**Publication:** Money magazine
**Title:** "Arguably the greatest global stock picker of the century"

A comprehensive profile that included extensive conversations with Templeton about his career. Key quotes:
> "To find the best investments, ask not where the outlook is brightest, but where the outlook is most miserable."

> "The time to sell is when you have found a much better bargain to replace it."

---

## 5. Charlie Rose Show (2005)

**Format:** Television interview
**Source:** PBS

In this later interview, Templeton discussed:
- His 2005 memo predicting financial chaos within 5 years (published posthumously in 2010)
- Prediction of housing market collapse and near-zero government bond yields
- Prediction of decline in traditional schooling due to internet-based learning
- Philanthropy and the Templeton Prize

## 6. Lauren Templeton — Nephew's Perspective

**Format:** Book and interviews
**Source:** *Investing the Templeton Way* (2008)

Lauren Templeton, Templeton's great-niece, wrote the definitive book on his investment philosophy. Her interviews about the book provide insights into:
- Templeton's personal habits and discipline
- How he identified bargains
- His checklist approach to stock selection
- His emphasis on research and independent thinking

## Summary of Conversational Themes

1. **Maximum pessimism is opportunity** — The emotional lows are when bargains are found
2. **Global scope** — Don't limit yourself to one market
3. **Bargain hunting discipline** — Systematic search for value everywhere
4. **Independence from the crowd** — Most dangerous words: "This time it's different"
5. **Patience and long-term thinking** — Hold until value is realized
6. **Humility** — No one knows everything
7. **Philanthropy** — The ultimate purpose of wealth
