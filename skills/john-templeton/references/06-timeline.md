# John Templeton — Timeline

---

## 1912–1936: Education & Early Life

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1912** | Born November 29 in Winchester, Tennessee |
| **1930** | Enters Yale University; finances through scholarship + poker winnings |
| **1934** | Graduates Yale near top of class; Phi Beta Kappa president |
| **1934–1936** | Rhodes Scholar at Balliol College, Oxford; M.A. in Law |
| **1936** | Travels to 27 countries across Europe and Asia |

---

## 1937–1953: Wall Street Career

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1936** | Returns to US; joins Fenner & Beane (predecessor to Merrill Lynch) |
| **1937** | Co-founds his own investment advisory firm |
| **1937** | Marries Judith Folk |
| **1939** | The defining trade: buys 104 stocks under $1 at WWII outbreak |
| **1940** | Purchases small investment advisory firm ($2M AUM) |
| **1951** | Judith Templeton dies in motorcycle accident |

---

## 1954–1992: Templeton Growth Fund Era

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1954** | Launches Templeton Growth Fund in Canada |
| **1958** | Marries Irene Reynolds Butler |
| **1959** | Firm goes public; 5 funds, $66M AUM |
| **1960s** | Pioneers Japanese stock investing |
| **1964** | Renounces US citizenship; becomes British subject |
| **1968** | Moves to Nassau, Bahamas |
| **1972** | Establishes Templeton Prize (£1M+, set above Nobel) |
| **1980s** | Continues global expansion; invests in emerging markets |
| **1987** | Knighted by Queen Elizabeth II |
| **1992** | Sells Templeton funds to Franklin Resources for $913M |

---

## 1993–2008: Philanthropy & Retirement

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1993** | Irene Templeton dies |
| **1999** | Money magazine: "Greatest global stock picker of the century" |
| **2004** | Publishes *21 Steps to Investment Success* |
| **2005** | Writes private memo predicting financial crisis within 5 years |
| **2007** | Time 100 list (Power Givers) |
| **2008** | Dies July 8 in Nassau, Bahamas, age 95 |
