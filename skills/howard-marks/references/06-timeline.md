# 06 — Timeline

A complete timeline of Howard Marks's life and career.

---

## Early Life

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1946 | Born April 23 in Queens, New York City. Raised as a Christian Scientist in a Jewish family. |
| 1967 | Graduated Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania (B.S.Ec. cum laude, major in finance) |
| 1969 | Earned MBA in Accounting and Marketing from University of Chicago Booth School of Business. Won the George Hay Brown Prize. |

---

## Citicorp (1969-1985)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1969 | Joined Citicorp as equity research analyst |
| 1975 | Earned CFA charterholder designation |
| 1978 | Promoted to Vice President and senior portfolio manager at Citicorp Investment Management. Charge of convertible and high-yield securities. **A phone call that year set him on the path to a career in bonds.** |
| 1978-1983 | Served as Director of Research at Citicorp |
| 1980 | Relocated to Los Angeles to manage a high-yield fund for Citibank |
| 1985 | Left Citicorp after 16 years |

---

## TCW Group (1985-1995)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1985 | Joined TCW Group, leading groups responsible for distressed debt, high-yield bonds, and convertible securities |
| 1988 | With Bruce Karsh, organized one of the first distressed debt funds from a major financial institution |
| 1990 | Published his **first investment memo** to clients (Oct 12, 1990). This began a practice that would continue for 35+ years. |

---

## Oaktree Capital Management (1995-Present)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 1995 | Co-founded Oaktree Capital Management with Bruce Karsh, Sheldon Stone, Richard Masson, and Larry Keele after TCW refused their proposal to manage funds independently while sharing fees |
| 1995 | Oaktree began operations focusing on high-yield debt, distressed debt, and private equity |
| 1998 | Started writing "The Memo by Howard Marks" podcast (retrospectively)
| 2000 | Memo "bubble.com" — called out the dot-com mania. Made five highly successful market calls on Jan 1, 2000 (defensive pivot) |

### 2000-2010

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2000-2010 | Chaired the University of Pennsylvania Investment Board (Trustees' Investment Board) |
| 2003 | Published "The Most Important Thing" memo (July 1, 2003) — the foundation for his later book |
| 2007 | "It's All Good" memo — warned of excessive optimism pre-financial crisis |
| 2008 | "The Limits of Negativism" — guided investors to be opportunistic during the panic |
| 2008 | Oaktree raised **$10.9 billion** during the crisis — the largest distressed debt fund in history |
| 2009 | "Nobody Knows II" — humbly acknowledging uncertainty during recovery |

### 2010-2020

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2011 | Published *The Most Important Thing: Uncommon Sense for the Thoughtful Investor* — Warren Buffett provided a blurb |
| 2012 | Oaktree IPO on NYSE (April 2012), raised $380 million |
| 2013 | Published *The Most Important Thing Illuminated* — annotated edition with contributions from Klarman, Greenblatt, Davis |
| 2017 | Memo "I Beg to Differ" — warned investors against cryptocurrency speculation |
| 2018 | Published *Mastering the Market Cycle: Getting the Odds on Your Side* |
| 2019 | Brookfield Asset Management acquired 62% of Oaktree. Marks and Oaktree team retained 38% and full operational control. |
| 2020 | COVID-19 — Oaktree deployed capital into distressed debt |
| 2020 | Memos: "The Value of Uncertainty" and "Calibrating" |

### 2020-Present

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| 2023 | Memo "Sea Change" — argued the era of low interest rates had structurally ended |
| 2024 | Memo "The Folly of Certainty" — on humility, uncertainty, and the danger of overconfidence |
| 2025 | Multiple media appearances: CNBC (Oct), Bloomberg Talks (Aug), William Green interview (Dec) |
| 2025 | Memo "Revisiting the Rejection of Economic Laws" — critique of government economic intervention |
| 2025 | Public "Don't Bet China Losing" statement |
| 2025 | Dialogue with Chen Guangming of Ruyuan Fund |
| 2026 | Appeared on Nikhil Kamath's "People by WTF" podcast (Apr 1) |
| 2026 | Wharton School Investor Series interview (Apr 21) |
| 2026 | Multiple TV appearances discussing AI's transformative potential (CNBC, Bloomberg, Mar 2026) |
| 2026 | Oaktree Conference — client conference reflections published |

## Key Milestones

- **1969-2026:** 57+ years of continuous investing career
- **1990-2026:** 36+ years of writing investment memos
- **1995-2026:** 31+ years building Oaktree from startup to $224B+ AUM
- **Net worth:** $2.2 billion (Forbes, 2022)
- **One of the longest continuous track records** among active institutional investors

---

## Personal Life

- Married to Nancy Freeman Marks
- Son: Andrew Marks (venture capitalist, TQ Ventures)
- Step-daughter: Jane Hait (founder, Center for Art, Research and Alliances)
- Residences: New York (740 Park Ave), Beverly Hills, East Hampton, Amagansett, Hawaii (Kukio)
- Board/Trustee: Metropolitan Museum of Art (Investment Committee), Royal Drawing School (London), Brookfield Corporation
- Philanthropy: Howard S. Marks Terms Scholarship (UPenn), Marks Family Writing Center (UPenn), UCLA Women's Health Research chair
- Current age: 80 (born 1946)
