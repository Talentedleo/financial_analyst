# 06-timeline.md — Ed Thorp's Life & Career Timeline

---

## Early Life & Education (1932-1958)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1932** | Born August 14 in Chicago, Illinois, during the Great Depression |
| **c. 1944** | Became one of the youngest licensed amateur radio operators at age 12 |
| **1940s** | Moved to Southern California; excelled in chemistry and physics competitions, winning scholarships |
| **c. 1950** | Attended UC Berkeley for undergraduate studies, transferred to UCLA after one year |
| **1955** | Met Vivian during his first year at UCLA |
| **1956** | Married Vivian in January |
| **1958** | Received Ph.D. in Mathematics from UCLA — dissertation: "Compact Linear Operators in Normed Spaces" under advisor Angus E. Taylor |

---

## MIT & Discovery of Card Counting (1959-1961)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1959** | Joined MIT as C.L.E. Moore Instructor (1959-1961) |
| **1960** | First Vegas trip with basic strategy card; lost, but realized the game could be beaten |
| **1960** | Collaborated with Claude Shannon on wearable computer for roulette prediction |
| **1961** | Used IBM 704 supercomputer at MIT (learned Fortran) to calculate blackjack probabilities |
| **1961** | Discovered that removing 5s from the deck gives the player the biggest edge |
| **1961** | Published "A favorable strategy for twenty-one" in *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* |
| **1961** | Manny Kimmel arrived in blue Cadillac with $10,000 stake; tested in Reno — won $11,000 in one weekend |

---

## Blackjack Fame & *Beat the Dealer* (1962-1966)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1962** | Published *Beat the Dealer* — NYT Bestseller, sold 700,000+ copies |
| **1962-66** | Regularly visited Vegas using disguises (false beard, wraparound glasses); was banned from casinos |
| **1962-66** | Professor at New Mexico State University (Mathematics, 1961-1965) |
| **1964** | Appeared on "To Tell The Truth" TV game show |
| **1965** | Joined UC Irvine as founding professor of mathematics |
| **1966** | Substantially updated *Beat the Dealer* (second edition) |

---

## Wall Street: *Beat the Market* & Princeton Newport Partners (1967-1989)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1967** | Published *Beat the Market* with Sheen T. Kassouf — warrant hedging formula |
| **1969** | Founded Convertible Hedge Associates (CHA) with Jay Regan — first market-neutral hedge fund |
| **1974** | CHA renamed to Princeton/Newport Partners (PNP) |
| **1975-1988** | PNP achieves 20% annualized returns, 227/230 months profitable, zero down quarters |
| **1977** | Published *Elementary Probability* textbook |
| **1982** | Retired from full-time teaching at UC Irvine |
| **1984** | Published *The Mathematics of Gambling* |
| **c. 1970s** | Played bridge with Warren Buffett |
| **1989** | PNP forced to liquidate due to RICO investigation from Drexel/Milken scandal — Thorp not charged |

---

## Later Career & The Madoff Detection (1990-2002)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1991** | Detected mathematical impossibility of Bernie Madoff's returns; warned friends |
| **1994** | Founded Ridgeline Partners — statistical arbitrage hedge fund |
| **1997** | Presented "The Kelly Criterion in Blackjack, Sports Betting, and the Stock Market" at 10th International Conference on Gambling and Risk Taking |
| **1998** | Reported personal investments yielded 20% annualized over 28.5 years |
| **2002** | Closed Ridgeline Partners due to declining returns in statistical arbitrage |

---

## Retirement & Legacy (2003-Present)

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| **2005** | Poundstone's *Fortune's Formula* published — definitive account of Kelly criterion |
| **2007** | Published "Understanding Fortune's Formula" — definitive explanation of Kelly criterion |
| **2008** | Madoff fraud publicly exposed; Thorp's 1991 warnings vindicated |
| **2008** | *Breaking Vegas* documentary aired on Discovery Channel |
| **2010** | Patterson's *The Quants* published — Chapter 2: "The Godfather: Ed Thorp" |
| **2011** | Co-edited *The Kelly Capital Growth Investment Criterion: Theory and Practice* |
| **2017** | Published autobiography *A Man for All Markets: From Las Vegas to Wall Street, How I Beat the Dealer and the Market* (age 85) |
| **2017** | Major media tour: NPR Planet Money, The Investor's Podcast, CNBC, WSJ, NYT, Bloomberg |
| **2018** | Donated personal and professional papers to UC Irvine Libraries |
| **2025** | Referenced in Chinese financial media; 40-year no-losing-year track record cited |
| **2026** | Age 93 — living in Newport Beach, California; official website maintained |
| **2026** | TIP128 (2017) remains the most comprehensive audio interview; republished on The Investor's Podcast |

## The 8 Breakthroughs (Summary)

| # | Breakthrough | Year | Impact |
|---|-------------|------|--------|
| 1 | Card counting system | 1961 | Forever changed blackjack; launched advantage gambling |
| 2 | Wearable computer (with Shannon) | 1961 | First wearable computing device |
| 3 | *Beat the Dealer* | 1962 | Bestseller; taught the world to count cards |
| 4 | Warrant hedging formula | 1967 | Anticipated Black-Scholes; launched quant finance |
| 5 | First market-neutral hedge fund | 1969 | PNP: 20% returns for 20+ years |
| 6 | Madoff detection | 1991 | Identified fraud 17 years early |
| 7 | Statistical arbitrage | 1994 | Ridgeline Partners |
| 8 | Kelly criterion framework | 1962-2011 | Optimal bet sizing for gambling and investing |
