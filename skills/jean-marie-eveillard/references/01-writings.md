# Jean-Marie Eveillard — Writings & Investment Philosophy

---

## 1. The SoGen / First Eagle Global Fund Track Record

**Fund:** SoGen International Fund (est. 1970) → First Eagle Global Fund (2000)
**Manager:** Jean-Marie Eveillard (1979–2004; returned 2007–2009)
**AUM Growth:** $15 million (1979) → ~$50 billion (2019)
**Returns:** Outperformed global indices consistently over 25+ years

**Key Innovation:** One of the first truly global value funds — go-anywhere approach investing in undervalued securities across all markets, all asset classes.

---

## 2. Core Investment Philosophy

### Capital Preservation Above All
> "The so-called margin of safety, with the Graham approach, is in the discount to intrinsic value. The larger the discount, the better."

Eveillard's primary objective: **avoid permanent capital loss**. This supersedes all else.

### "Not to Lose Money"
His famous dictum — deceptively simple, requiring immense discipline. Focus on avoiding permanent loss takes precedence over pursuing high-flying gains.

### Bottom-Up Selection + Top-Down Awareness
- Rigorous fundamental analysis of individual companies
- Complements with macroeconomic awareness (credit cycles, geopolitics)
- But bottom-up dominates: "five or six strengths and weaknesses of the business and ignoring everything else"

### Gold as Permanent Portfolio Insurance
- Eveillard held 5–15% in gold and gold-related investments permanently
- Not as a bet on price appreciation, but as **systematic hedge** against currency debasement and systemic risk
- One of the most distinctive elements of his strategy

### Global Go-Anywhere Approach
- Unconstrained by geography, sector, or asset class
- Invests in US, Europe, Japan, emerging markets
- Holds equities, bonds, gold, cash — whatever offers the best risk-adjusted value

### Contrarian Discipline
- Refused to chase dot-com stocks despite intense pressure and fund outflows
- "If you're a value investor, you're a long-term investor"
- The market is a "voting machine" short-term, "weighing machine" long-term

---

## 3. Key Investment Principles (from Grokipedia/Notable People)

1. **Margin of Safety:** Buy at significant discount to intrinsic value
2. **Avoid Permanent Capital Loss:** The only risk that matters
3. **Long-Term Holding:** Patience is essential; hold through underperformance
4. **Global Diversification:** Search for value everywhere
5. **Gold as Hedge:** Permanent 5–15% allocation
6. **Independent Thinking:** Ignore the crowd; follow facts
7. **Intellectual Humility:** Acknowledge what you don't know
8. **Strong Balance Sheets:** Prefer companies with financial strength

- **Columbia Business School:** William von Mueffling Professor of Professional Practice

- **HEC Paris:** Endowed a chair in behavioral finance and value investing (2019)

---

## Summary of Core Contributions

1. **Extreme Risk Aversion:** Demonstrated that capital preservation is the foundation of long-term outperformance
2. **Gold as Permanent Hedge:** First major institutional investor to systematically allocate to gold
3. **Go-Anywhere Global Value:** Pioneered truly unconstrained global value investing
4. **Dot-Com Discipline:** Refused to chase tech stocks despite intense pressure — legendary case study in conviction
5. **Academic Legacy:** Teaching at Columbia and endowing the HEC chair ensures his philosophy lives on
