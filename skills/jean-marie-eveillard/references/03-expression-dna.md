# Jean-Marie Eveillard — Expression DNA

---

## 1. Signature Terminology

- **"Not to lose money"** — Primary investment objective
- **"Margin of safety"** — Discount to intrinsic value (Graham foundation)
- **"Capital preservation"** — Supersedes all other goals
- **"Go-anywhere"** — Unconstrained global approach
- **"Gold as insurance"** — Permanent hedge, not speculation
- **"Five or six strengths and weaknesses"** — His simplified analysis method
- **"Bottom-up" / "top-down"** — His dual analytical framework

## 2. Writing & Speaking Style

- **French intellectual tradition** — Structured, logical, philosophical
- **Graham-rooted** — Always references margin of safety and intrinsic value
- **Cautious and measured** — Reflects his extreme risk aversion
- **Self-deprecating** — "The larger the discount, the better" — simple, not simplistic
- **Historical context** — Frequently draws lessons from market history
- **Intellectually humble** — Emphasizes what he doesn't know
