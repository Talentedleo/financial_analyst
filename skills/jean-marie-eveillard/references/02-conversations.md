# Jean-Marie Eveillard — Conversations & Interviews

---

## 1. Morningstar Awards & Interviews (Early 2000s)

**Context:** Eveillard received Morningstar's International Stock Manager of the Year (2001) and Lifetime Achievement Award (2003). These awards included interview opportunities where he explained his philosophy.

**Key quotes from this era:**
- "The so-called margin of safety... the larger the discount, the better"
- On avoiding tech stocks: "If you are a value investor, you are a long-term investor"
- "Not to lose money" as the primary objective

---

## 2. Wall Street Transcript Interview (March 2003)

**Format:** In-depth investment interview
**Source:** The Wall Street Transcript

Covered his global value approach, gold allocation rationale, and views on international markets at the time.

## 3. Columbia Business School Teaching (2010s)

**Format:** Course instruction — "Value Investing with Legends"
**Source:** Columbia Business School, Heilbrunn Center

Co-taught with Bruce Greenwald and Tano Santos. While not a published interview, his lectures are legendary among Columbia MBA students.

## 4. Later Career Commentary

After retirement, Eveillard occasionally participated in:
- Industry panel discussions
- Academic conferences
- First Eagle investment commentary

His commentaries are characterized by clarity, historical perspective, and unwavering focus on fundamental principles.

## 5. Silba Deep Dives — "Everything Is in the Books" (2025–2026)

**Format:** Comprehensive biographical essay (Substack)
**Source:** [Silba Deep Dives](https://silbadeepdives.substack.com/p/jean-marie-eveillard)

**Key Revelations:**
- His teacher's parting advice: "Never forget, everything is in the books"
- The "conversion" to Graham in 1968 — described as a religious illumination
- Ten years of frustration at Société Générale: "My first fifteen years at the bank were a waste"
- Rejected DCF as "unusable in practice"
- Early portfolio returns: 188% in first 5 years vs 78% for MSCI World
- Defined value investing as "a big tent" — Graham on one end, Buffett on the other

### Key Quotes from Silba Portrait
> "In a sense, I was illuminated not by faith, but all of a sudden, it seemed to me that Ben Graham simply made sense."

> "The first fifteen years at the bank were a waste, with the first five spent not knowing how to go about investing and the next ten being unable to use the value investing approach of Ben Graham."

> On DCF: "Unusable in practice"

## Summary of Conversational Themes

1. **Avoid losing money** — "Not to lose money" is the first and most important rule
2. **Margin of safety** — Discount to intrinsic value is the foundation
3. **Gold as insurance** — Permanent hedge, nothing more
4. **Patience** — Accept underperformance as the price of discipline
5. **Independent thinking** — Don't follow the crowd
6. **Humility** — Recognize what you don't know
