# 04-external-views.md — How Others View Mohnish Pabrai

---

## 1. Media and Profiles

### Smallcase
- "One of the most successful disciples of Warren Buffett's value investing philosophy"
- "Renowned value investor" with 517% net returns vs. 43% for S&P 500

### Value Investors Community
- Pabrai is widely respected within the value investing community
- His cloning strategy is both admired and debated
- His willingness to admit mistakes openly is considered rare

### Guy Spier (Aquamarine Capital)
- Close friend of Pabrai; they shared a famous $650k charity lunch with Buffett
- Spier's book *The Education of a Value Investor* discusses their friendship
- Both are known as "Buffett clones"

---

## 2. The Buffett Lunch (2008)

Pabrai and Guy Spier paid $650,100 for a charity lunch with Warren Buffett.

**Significance:**
- Demonstrated Pabrai's commitment to learning from the best
- The lunch reinforced his cloning philosophy
- The story is often cited as evidence of Pabrai's willingness to invest in learning

---

## 3. Critics

### Cloning Debate
- Critics argue cloning is intellectually lazy and fails when market conditions change
- Pabrai counters that cloning is rational — it's learning from proven success
- The debate continues within value investing circles

### Horsehead Investment
- A major failure that drew significant criticism
- Pabrai lost substantial capital and has discussed it openly in interviews
- Used as a case study in the dangers of commodity businesses

---

## 4. Legacy

- **The Dhandho Investor:** One of the most accessible value investing books
- **Cloning as a Legitimate Strategy:** Made imitation intellectually respectable
- **Checklist Investing:** Popularized Munger's checklist concept for individual investors
- **Fee Innovation:** Cloned Buffett's no-management-fee partnership structure
- **Value Investing Ambassador:** Bridge between Buffett/Munger and a new generation of investors
