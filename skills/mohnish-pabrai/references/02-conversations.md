## 5. Key Interview Themes

1. **Cloning is rational:** "Innovation is overrated. Copying proven strategies is smart"
2. **Checklist saves you from yourself:** Formal processes prevent emotional decisions
3. **Concentration is the only path to outsized returns:** "Few bets, big bets, infrequent bets"
4. **Mistakes are tuition:** Every error teaches something valuable
5. **The Buffett fee model is the right one:** No management fee, performance fee only
6. **Patience is the real edge:** "The stock market is a device for transferring money from the impatient to the patient"

## 8. The Buffett Charity Lunch (2008)

**Context:** Pabrai and Guy Spier won the bid for a private lunch with Warren Buffett.

**Key Details:**
- Price: $650,100
- Winner: Pabrai and Spier (shared)
- Three-hour lunch at Smith & Wollensky in New York
- "The best money I ever spent on education"

**Impact:**
- Direct exposure to Buffett's thinking and personality
- Reinforced Pabrai's cloning philosophy — hearing it from the source
- Became a foundational story in Pabrai's investing career
