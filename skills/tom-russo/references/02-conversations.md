## 1. Latticework 2025 — Tom Russo on Finding Global Compounders (2025)

**Format:** Keynote presentation + Q&A
**Host:** MOI Global (Latticework)
**Source:** [Latticework by MOI Global](https://www.latticework.com/p/latticework-2025-tom-russo-on-finding)

## 6. Meeting Buffett & Munger at Stanford (1983)

Russo frequently recounts his first meeting with Warren Buffett and Charlie Munger at Stanford Business School:
> "Warren's first words when he showed up to our class, Jack McDonald's class at Value Investing, was that there's only one break that the government gives investors. So make sure you process that as part of your objective. And that's the non-taxation of unrealized gains."

This lesson shaped his entire career — sub-5% turnover, long holding periods, tax-efficient compounding.

---

## Summary of Conversational Themes

1. **Capacity to suffer** — Management's willingness to absorb short-term pain for long-term gain
2. **Weetabix lesson** — The power of buybacks when reinvestment is limited
3. **Family-controlled companies** — Governance as competitive advantage
4. **Global consumer brands** — Dominant franchises with pricing power
5. **Low turnover** — Sub-5%, driven by tax efficiency
6. **Strange market** — Current environment is "as weird as I can ever remember"
