# Tom Russo — Expression DNA

---

## 1. Signature Terminology

- **"Capacity to suffer"** — Management's willingness to endure short-term pain
- **"Capacity to reinvest"** — Ability to absorb capital internally
- **"No across the street"** — No adequate substitute (Buffett's phrase)
- **"As weird as I can ever remember"** — Current market description
- **"Non-taxation of unrealized gains"** — The single break from the government
- **"Family-controlled"** — Preferred governance structure
- **"Global consumer franchise"** — His preferred business type

## 2. Writing & Speaking Style

- **Conversational and self-deprecating** — "Are you kidding? How can you own spirits?"
- **Anecdotal** — Uses case studies (Weetabix, Yoplait) to illustrate principles
- **Straightforward** — No jargon; explains complex ideas simply
- **Patient** — Reflects his long-term orientation
- **Respectful of Buffett/Munger** — Frequently references their lessons
